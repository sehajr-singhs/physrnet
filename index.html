PSN-1

Physics Systems Network with Equivariant Attention and Conservation Law Discovery
Sehaj Singh
NMI Paper IEEE Paper GitHub
PSN-1 unifies E(3)-equivariant message passing, attention-based reasoning, and conservation law discovery in a single gated architecture. The model achieves machine-precision equivariance (<10⁻⁷), energy conservation errors below 10⁻⁵, and test MSE as low as 3.4×10⁻¹¹ for 4-body gravity. A learned gate adaptively balances architectural symmetry priors with data-driven attention.

Architecture

  • Module 1: E(3)-Equivariant Encoder — EGNN-style message passing with rotation-invariant scalars and rotation-equivariant vectors. Guarantees exact E(3) symmetry.
  • Module 2: Attention Reasoning GNN — Multi-head attention discovers interaction types. Each head captures distinct physics (attractive, repulsive, long-range).
  • Module 3: Conservation Discovery — Learns to predict energy, momentum, and angular momentum from particle states without explicit supervision.
  • Module 4: PINN Loss — Physics-informed residuals for energy/momentum conservation as auxiliary training objective.
  • Learned Gate — Per-node gate blends equivariant and attention pathways: a = g·a_eq + (1-g)·a_attn

Conservation Law Discovery

The conservation discovery module learns to predict total energy, momentum, and angular momentum from particle states. On gravity, the predicted energy correlates with ground truth at r² > 0.999, demonstrating automatic discovery of the conserved quantity. The PINN loss further improves conservation: energy error drops from 6.1×10⁻⁶ (no PINN) to 4.3×10⁻⁶ (with PINN).

Key Findings

  • Exact equivariance is maintained through architectural constraints, not learned
  • The learned gate adapts to system complexity: simple systems → balanced, complex → equivariant-dominant
  • Attention heads discover interpretable interaction patterns that correspond to physical forces
  • Conservation loss reduces trajectory drift by up to 30%
  • The model generalizes across particle masses and initial conditions
