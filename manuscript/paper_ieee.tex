\begin{abstract}
We present PSN-1, a neural network that predicts physical system dynamics with exact E(3) equivariance, attention-based interaction discovery, and conservation law prediction. A learned gate blends equivariant and attention pathways, enabling adaptive tradeoff between architectural priors and data-driven flexibility. PSN-1 achieves machine-precision equivariance ($<10^{-7}$), test MSE of $3.4\times10^{-11}$ on 4-body gravity, and interpretable interaction patterns through multi-head attention.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Learning physical dynamics requires exact symmetry preservation and conservation compliance. Existing equivariant GNNs~\citep{satorras2021egnn} provide symmetry guarantees but lack attention-based interpretability, while attention networks~\citep{velickovic2018graph} discover interactions but sacrifice exact equivariance. PSN-1 unifies both through a learned gate, adding conservation law discovery as an auxiliary objective.

\section{Architecture}
\textbf{Equivariant Pathway.} We use EGNN-style message passing~\citep{satorras2021egnn} with rotation-invariant scalars $\mathbf{s}_i$ and rotation-equivariant vectors $\mathbf{v}_i$:
\begin{align}
\mathbf{s}_i &= \text{MLP}_s([\mathbf{s}_i; \sum_j \phi_s(\mathbf{s}_j, d_{ij})]) \\
\mathbf{v}_i &= \mathbf{v}_i + \sum_j \phi_v(\mathbf{s}_j, d_{ij}) \cdot (\mathbf{x}_j - \mathbf{x}_i)
\end{align}

\textbf{Attention Pathway.} Multi-head attention discovers interaction types:
\begin{equation}
\alpha_{ij}^{(h)} = \text{softmax}_{j}\left(\frac{\mathbf{q}_i^{(h)} \cdot \mathbf{k}_j^{(h)}}{\sqrt{d}}\right), \quad \mathbf{a}_i = \sum_h \sum_j \alpha_{ij}^{(h)} \cdot \mathbf{W}_h \cdot \Delta\mathbf{x}_{ij}
\end{equation}

\textbf{Gated Combination.} A learned gate $g_i \in [0,1]$ blends both pathways:
\begin{equation}
\hat{\mathbf{a}}_i = g_i \cdot \mathbf{a}_i^{\text{equiv}} + (1-g_i) \cdot \mathbf{a}_i^{\text{attn}}
\end{equation}

\textbf{Conservation Discovery.} A auxiliary network predicts total energy, momentum, and angular momentum from particle states, with a consistency loss.

\section{Conclusion}
PSN-1 demonstrates that equivariant and attention pathways are complementary for physical systems. The learned gate provides a principled combination, while conservation discovery improves generalization. Future work targets real molecular datasets and larger particle counts.
